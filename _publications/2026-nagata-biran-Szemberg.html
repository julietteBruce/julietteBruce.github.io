--- layout: publication year: 2026 title: "A Tropical Viewpoint on the Nagata--Biran--Szemberg Conjecture" authors: - Juliette Bruce doi: arxiv: "2610.41931" status: - Pre-Print type: - Research journal: journal_url: tags: - Algebraic Geometry - Tropical Geometry pdf: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.41931 highlight: true --- We give a tropical perspective on the recent arguments announced by OpenAI for Nagata's conjecture and the Nagata--Biran--Szemberg conjecture. By degenerating to both the normal cone and a semistable model whose dual graph is a cycle, we replace sections on a surface by Laurent polynomials in two variables. In place of theta functions, tropical methods bound the exponents of these Laurent polynomials, and a lift of the tropical Abel--Jacobi class of the normal bundle controls the shape of the resulting exponent diagram. Combining these support bounds with interpolation on a torus proves the Nagata--Biran--Szemberg conjecture with its predicted threshold whenever the corresponding linear series contains an integral curve whose only singularity is an ordinary node that is not a basepoint.
